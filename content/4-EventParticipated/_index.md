# Events Participated

During my internship, I participated in three cloud and AI community events in Ho Chi Minh City. Each event provided practical perspectives from industry engineers, Solution Architects, and AWS leadership, helping shape my engineering mindset around system resilience, Agentic AI in production, security, and cost awareness.

### [Event 1 — First Cloud AI Journey](4.1-Event1/)
&emsp;**Event Name:** First Cloud AI Journey Meetup  
&emsp;**Date & Time:** 09:00 – 12:00, Saturday, October 24, 2026  
&emsp;**Location:** 26th Floor, Bitexco Financial Tower, Ho Chi Minh City  
&emsp;**Role:** Attendee  
&emsp;**Summary:** Five back-to-back sessions on Cloud and AI Agents covering Vietnamese Voice AI on AWS, cross-account AWS Organization boundaries, shifting IaC security left, Amazon Bedrock AgentCore memory management, and continuous AI Agents with Kiro Crew.

### [Event 2 — AWS Cloud & AI Day 2026: HCMC Watch Party](4.2-Event2/)
&emsp;**Event Name:** AWS Cloud & AI Day 2026: HCMC Watch Party  
&emsp;**Date & Time:** 2026 (Full-day Watch Party)  
&emsp;**Location:** AWS Office, Ho Chi Minh City (hosted by the AWS Study Group community)  
&emsp;**Role:** Attendee  
&emsp;**Summary:** Community Watch Party with over 300 builders featuring keynotes on Agentic AI, data foundations, and enterprise cloud modernization, followed by candid roundtable discussions with Solution Architects and developers on moving AI agents to production and optimizing cloud costs.
